#  FLOOR 13

### A First-Person Psychological Horror Game

*Some floors were never meant to exist.*

**Floor 13** is a single-player, first-person psychological horror game developed in Unity. You play as a new tenant moving into Apartment 401, where an ordinary apartment building slowly reveals something deeply unsettling about its elevator.

What begins as a normal move-in soon turns into a disturbing mystery involving a missing floor, strange encounters, and secrets hidden throughout the building.

Can you uncover the truth behind Floor 13?

---

##   Game Overview

* **Genre:** Psychological Horror, Mystery, Exploration
* **Perspective:** First-Person
* **Game Mode:** Single-Player
* **Engine:** Unity 6
* **Render Pipeline:** Universal Render Pipeline (URP)
* **3D Modeling:** Blender
* **Development:** Solo Project
* **Status:** In Development

##  Story

You have just moved into Apartment 401.

Your first night is simple: move your belongings, explore the building, and settle into your new home.

But things begin to change.

A mysterious button marked **13** appears inside the elevator. The building directory lists only twelve floors. The receptionist seems to know more than he lets on, and strange events begin to unfold as the nights pass.

As you investigate, you discover traces of people who encountered the same mystery before you.

The deeper you go, the more you begin to question the building itself.

**Floor 13 is not just another floor.**

##  Features

* **First-Person Exploration** — Explore an atmospheric apartment building from an immersive first-person perspective.
* **Interactive Elevator System** — Operate elevator buttons, doors, floor displays, and lighting.
* **Object Interaction** — Pick up, carry, and deliver objects as part of gameplay objectives.
* **Branching Dialogue** — Interact with characters through conversations and player choices.
* **Story-Driven Objectives** — Complete tasks and uncover clues as the story progresses.
* **Dynamic Story Events** — Experience changes in the environment and narrative as the nights advance.
* **Atmospheric Horror** — Discover unsettling anomalies through environmental storytelling and unexpected events.

*Features are being implemented and expanded throughout development.*

##  Technical Highlights

Floor 13 is being developed with a focus on modular, reusable gameplay systems.

Current development areas include:

* Elevator management, floor selection, door animations, and display updates.
* Interactive object pickup and delivery mechanics.
* Objective tracking and user interface.
* Dialogue management with branching choices, story flags, and dialogue history.
* NPC interactions and proximity-based character behavior.
* Night-based progression and scripted narrative events.
